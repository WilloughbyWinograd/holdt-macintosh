## Immediate Cautions

1. Do not leave the Macintosh SE powered unattended.
2. Do not repeatedly power cycle it for curiosity before the battery and hard disk preservation path are addressed.
3. Do not connect or disconnect Apple Desktop Bus devices while the Macintosh is powered on.
4. Do not install new software on the internal hard drive until its contents are copied.
5. Do not open the Cathode Ray Tube side casually. Compact Macintosh computers can retain dangerous high voltage.
