# 2026-06-01 Holdt Context

The Team 6502 repository identifies Terry Holdt as Jennifer Holdt Winograd's father and Willoughby's maternal grandfather.

Relevant repository sources:

1. `about.html`: Jennifer Holdt Winograd describes how Team 6502 came to be and identifies herself as the daughter of Terry Holdt.
2. `terry-holdt.html`: Terry Holdt is described as the Product and Program Manager for Microcomputers at MOS Technology and the 6502 Process Development Manager.
3. `the-holdt-archives.html`: The archives describe Terry Holdt's role in the Motorola to MOS Technology move and in the production and process work around the MOS Technology 6502 microprocessor.

For this restoration repository, `holdt-macintosh` refers to that family and computing legacy.
